Controls

Tools for focused watching.

PopupX combines overlay playback, miniplayer controls, keyboard shortcuts, and distraction filters for YouTube-heavy workflows.

Overlay & sidebar

Open videos centered on the page or dock them to a side panel.

Context menu

Right-click links, images, pages, or videos to preview them in PopupX.

Distraction filters

Hide feeds, comments, shorts, sidebars, and other YouTube clutter.

Picture-in-Picture

Move playback into a floating browser PiP window when supported.

More controls

Cycle aspect ratios, sizes, labels, copy URLs, and open pages in tabs or windows.

Compatibility

Focused on YouTube previews.

PopupX only opens YouTube videos in the overlay. Links from other websites are ignored so the extension stays focused and avoids broad site access.

Good to know: YouTube watch pages, thumbnails, Shorts, search results, and supported YouTube embeds remain the intended preview targets.
