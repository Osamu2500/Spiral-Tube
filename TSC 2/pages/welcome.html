PopupX is ready to go

Watch any YouTube video in a floating player — overlay, sidebar, miniplayer, picture-in-picture, its own window, or Music Mode — without leaving the page you are on. To try it now, open YouTube and Alt + click or double-click a video.

Three ways to open a video

PopupX never adds buttons to thumbnails — it stays out of the way until you ask for it.

  1. 1
    Alt + click or double-click any video

    Works on thumbnails and links across the home feed, search, channels and playlists. Pick which one you prefer under Player → Open PopupX with.

  2. 2
    Right-click a video card

    PopupX's own menu opens it as a popup, miniplayer, picture-in-picture, new window or Music Mode — or copies the URL and grabs the thumbnail.

  3. 3
    Press Alt + S to search

    A floating search panel renders results in place, and Watch now plays them straight into the popup.

Hide the noise

Minimal Mode

Volume boost & screenshots

Push quiet videos up to 1000%, grab the current frame as an image, and redirect Shorts to the normal watch page.

Presets and backups

Start from Default, Compact, Cinema or Power user, save your own, and export everything to a JSON file.

Keyboard shortcuts

Available on any YouTube page.

  • Popup the focused or hovered video Alt+Shift+P
  • Open floating search Alt+S
  • Toggle the miniplayer Alt+Shift+M
  • Close the popup Alt+Shift+X
  • While the popup is focused: close it, or dock it to the miniplayer Esc / M

Shortcuts can be remapped from your browser's extension shortcuts page (chrome://extensions/shortcuts). Everything else lives in Customize options.
